              How does the bot work, and how does it protect me?
              
    
                
  • What it does: every few minutes during market hours it scores each stock with a model trained
                  on price action, market context and news sentiment, and buys when the odds look good.
    • 
                
  • Every trade has a stop-loss and take-profit at the broker, so positions stay protected even if
                  your computer turns off.
    • 
                
  • Day-trading mode closes everything before 4 pm ET: no overnight risk.
    • 
                
  • Loss limits: it stops opening trades after a 2% drop in a day, caps total risk across positions,
                  and pauses itself if live results fall well short of the test.
    • 
                
  • Honest warning: most automated day-trading strategies lose money after costs. The validation step
                  exists so the bot doesn't trade when there's no evidence of an edge. Never trade money you can't afford to lose.

API keys

            
Your keys are saved in the .env file on this computer only (readable by you
              alone). They are never shown again or sent anywhere except Alpaca.


            

              
How to get your Alpaca keys (about 5 minutes)

              
    
                
  1. Create a free account at alpaca.markets and log in.
    2. 
                
  2. Paper keys: in the top-left account switcher choose Paper Trading. On the home page find
                  API Keys and click Generate New Keys.
    3. 
                
  3. Copy the Key ID and the Secret Key into the boxes below. The secret is only shown once, so if
                  you lose it, just generate new keys.
    4. 
                
  4. Live keys (optional): only after your live account is funded and approved, switch to your
                  Live account and generate keys the same way. Paper keys start with PK, live keys usually with AK.
    5. 
              

              
Tip: never share these keys or paste them into chats or emails. Anyone with them can trade your account.
